### Database Schema & Relationship Overview

This database consists of three schemas: `public` (core transactional and master data), `analytics` (pre-engineered feature stores), and `kpi` (business intelligence views and aggregations).

---

### 1. Public Schema (Core Operational Tables)

- **`cities`**
  - Columns: `city_id` (SERIAL, PK), `name` (VARCHAR, UNIQUE)
  - Description: Stores geographic locations/cities.

- **`users`**
  - Columns: `user_id` (BIGINT, PK)
  - Description: Stores unique platform users.

- **`sessions`**
  - Columns: `session_id` (VARCHAR, PK), `user_id` (BIGINT, FK to `users.user_id`), `city_id` (INT, FK to `cities.city_id`)
  - Description: Tracks user browsing sessions and their associated city.

- **`brands`**
  - Columns: `brand_id` (SERIAL, PK), `name` (VARCHAR, UNIQUE)
  - Description: Stores product brands.

- **`categories`**
  - Columns: `category_id` (BIGSERIAL, PK), `category1` (VARCHAR), `category2` (VARCHAR), `sub_category` (VARCHAR)
  - Description: Hierarchical product categorization.

- **`sellers`**
  - Columns: `seller_id` (SERIAL, PK), `seller_title` (VARCHAR, UNIQUE)
  - Description: Stores product vendors or sellers.

- **`products`**
  - Columns: `id` (BIGINT, PK), `title_fa` (VARCHAR), `brand_id` (INT, FK to `brands.brand_id`), `category_id` (BIGINT, FK to `categories.category_id`), `seller_id` (INT, FK to `sellers.seller_id`), `price` (BIGINT), `min_price_last_month` (BIGINT), `is_fake` (BOOLEAN), `rate` (DOUBLE PRECISION), `rate_cnt` (BIGINT)
  - Description: Stores detailed catalog information for each product.

- **`user_behavior_logs`**
  - Columns: `log_id` (BIGSERIAL, PK), `session_id` (VARCHAR, FK to `sessions.session_id`), `product_id` (BIGINT, FK to `products.id`), `event_type` (VARCHAR - e.g., 'view', 'add_to_cart', 'remove_from_cart', 'purchase'), `timestamp` (TIMESTAMPTZ)
  - Description: Event stream capturing user interactions with products during sessions.

- **`comments`**
  - Columns: `id` (BIGINT, PK), `product_id` (BIGINT, FK to `products.id`), `is_buyer` (BOOLEAN), `rate` (DOUBLE PRECISION), `recommendation_status` (VARCHAR), `likes` (INT), `dislikes` (INT), `raw_text_normalized` (TEXT), `created_at` (TIMESTAMPTZ)
  - Description: User reviews and feedback on products.

- **`comments_embedding`**
  - Columns: `id` (BIGINT, PK, FK to `comments.id`), `embedded_comment` (VECTOR)
  - Description: Vector embeddings of comments for semantic search or analysis.

- **`comment_aspects`**
  - Columns: `aspect_id` (BIGSERIAL, PK), `comment_id` (BIGINT, FK to `comments.id`), `term` (VARCHAR), `sentiment` (VARCHAR - e.g., 'positive', 'negative', 'neutral'), `negative_pct` (DOUBLE PRECISION), `neutral_pct` (DOUBLE PRECISION), `positive_pct` (DOUBLE PRECISION)
  - Description: Aspect-Based Sentiment Analysis (ABSA) extracted from comments, detailing specific terms and sentiment scores.

---

### 2. Analytics Schema (Pre-Engineered Feature Tables)

- **`analytics.feature_behavior`**
  - Columns: `log_id`, `hour`, `day`, `month`, `weekday`, `is_weekend`, `is_view`, `is_cart`, `is_remove`, `is_purchase`
  - Description: Extracted temporal and binary flag features from individual behavior logs.

- **`analytics.feature_user`**
  - Columns: `user_id`, `total_events`, `total_sessions`, `active_days`, `total_views`, `total_cart_adds`, `total_removes`, `total_purchases`, `avg_session_events`, `max_session_events`, `avg_session_duration_minutes`, `max_session_duration_minutes`, `weekend_activity_ratio`, `morning_activity_ratio`, `afternoon_activity_ratio`, `evening_activity_ratio`, `night_activity_ratio`, `preferred_hour`, `preferred_weekday`, `unique_products_viewed`, `unique_products_purchased`, `cities_visited`, `total_spend`, `avg_purchase_value`, `min_purchase_price`, `max_purchase_price`, `purchase_frequency`, `purchase_days`, `brand_diversity`, `category_diversity`
  - Description: Aggregated behavioral, temporal, and financial profile features for each user.

- **`analytics.feature_product`**
  - Columns: `product_id`, `total_events`, `total_views`, `total_cart_adds`, `total_removes`, `total_purchases`, `unique_viewers`, `unique_carters`, `unique_buyers`, `total_sessions`, `price_drop_ratio`
  - Description: Aggregated interaction and pricing metrics per product.

- **`analytics.feature_city`**
  - Columns: `city_id`, `total_users`, `total_sessions`, `total_events`, `total_views`, `total_cart_adds`, `total_purchases`, `total_removes`, `unique_products_viewed`, `unique_products_purchased`
  - Description: Geographic performance and engagement metrics aggregated by city.

- **`analytics.feature_category`**
  - Columns: `category_id`, `total_events`, `total_views`, `total_cart_adds`, `total_purchases`, `total_removes`, `unique_viewers`, `unique_buyers`, `avg_product_price`
  - Description: Performance metrics and average pricing aggregated by product category.

- **`analytics.feature_brand`**
  - Columns: `brand_id`, `total_events`, `total_views`, `total_cart_adds`, `total_purchases`, `total_removes`, `unique_viewers`, `unique_buyers`
  - Description: Performance and engagement metrics aggregated by brand.

- **`analytics.feature_user_product`**
  - Columns: `user_id`, `product_id`, `total_events`, `view_count`, `cart_count`, `remove_count`, `purchase_count`, `active_days`, `session_count`
  - Description: Interaction history matrix between individual users and specific products.

- **`analytics.feature_user_category`**
  - Columns: `user_id`, `category_id`, `total_events`, `view_count`, `cart_count`, `remove_count`, `purchase_count`, `category_spend`, `view_share`, `purchase_share`, `spend_share`
  - Description: Category preference and share-of-wallet metrics per user.

- **`analytics.feature_product_sentiment`**
  - Columns: `product_id`, `comment_count`, `avg_rate`, `avg_like_ratio`, `total_likes`, `total_dislikes`, `total_aspect_mentions`, `positive_aspect_mentions`, `negative_aspect_mentions`, `neutral_aspect_mentions`, `avg_positive_pct`, `avg_negative_pct`, `avg_neutral_pct`, `positive_aspect_ratio`, `negative_aspect_ratio`, `neutral_aspect_ratio`
  - Description: Aggregated review ratings, likes, and aspect-based sentiment metrics per product.

- **`analytics.feature_product_aspect`**
  - Columns: `product_id`, `term`, `total_mentions`, `positive_mentions`, `negative_mentions`, `neutral_mentions`, `avg_negative_pct`, `avg_neutral_pct`, `avg_positive_pct`
  - Description: Detailed sentiment breakdown for specific aspect terms associated with individual products.

- **`analytics.feature_brand_sentiment`**
  - Columns: `brand_id`, `total_comments`, `total_aspect_mentions`, `positive_aspect_mentions`, `negative_aspect_mentions`, `neutral_aspect_mentions`, `avg_comment_rating`, `total_likes`, `total_dislikes`
  - Description: Aggregated sentiment and review metrics per brand.

- **`analytics.feature_category_sentiment`**
  - Columns: `category_id`, `total_comments`, `total_aspect_mentions`, `positive_aspect_mentions`, `negative_aspect_mentions`, `neutral_aspect_mentions`, `avg_comment_rating`, `total_likes`, `total_dislikes`
  - Description: Aggregated sentiment and review metrics per product category.

- **`analytics.feature_aspect`**
  - Columns: `term`, `total_mentions`, `positive_mentions`, `negative_mentions`, `neutral_mentions`, `avg_negative_pct`, `avg_neutral_pct`, `avg_positive_pct`
  - Description: Global aspect-level sentiment metrics across all products.

- **`analytics.feature_time`**
  - Columns: `hour`, `iso_weekday`, `total_events`, `total_views`, `total_cart_adds`, `total_purchases`, `total_removes`
  - Description: Activity metrics aggregated by hour of the day and day of the week.

### 3. KPI Schema (Business Intelligence Views & Aggregations)

- **`kpi.global_funnel`**
  - Columns: `total_views`, `total_carts`, `total_purchases`, `total_removes`, `view_to_cart_pct`, `cart_to_purchase_pct`, `overall_conversion_pct`, `cart_abandonment_pct`
  - Description: Macro-level conversion funnel metrics for the entire platform.

- **`kpi.product_360`**
  - Columns: `product_id`, `title_fa`, `price`, `total_views`, `total_purchases`, `total_revenue`, `conversion_rate`, `comment_count`, `star_rating`, `positive_sentiment_pct`, `sentiment_score`, `managerial_action_tag`
  - Description: Comprehensive view combining product sales, behavior, sentiment scores, and automated management tags (e.g., 'Hero Product', 'High Risk').

- **`kpi.user_segments`**
  - Columns: `user_id`, `active_days`, `total_views`, `total_purchases`, `total_spend`, `user_segment`, `user_conversion_pct`
  - Description: Behavioral and financial segmentation of users (e.g., 'VIP Customer', 'Returning Customer', 'Window Shopper').

- **`kpi.brand_diagnostics`**
  - Columns: `brand_id`, `brand_name`, `total_views`, `total_purchases`, `total_comments`, `avg_rating`, `brand_sentiment_score`
  - Description: Brand performance tracking combining engagement, sales, and sentiment scores.

- **`kpi.aspect_diagnostics`**
  - Columns: `aspect_name`, `total_mentions`, `positive_mentions`, `negative_mentions`, `negative_impact_pct`, `aspect_status`
  - Description: Root-cause analysis identifying critical product strengths and weaknesses based on aspect sentiments.

- **`kpi.rfm_segments`**
  - Columns: `user_id`, `recency_days`, `frequency`, `monetary`, `rfm_code`, `rfm_label`
  - Description: Recency, Frequency, Monetary scoring (NTILE 1-5) and classification labels ('vip', 'promising', 'at_risk', 'lost', 'regular').

- **`kpi.ml_user_clusters`**
  - Columns: `user_id` (PK, FK to `public.users.user_id`), `cluster_id`, `cluster_name`
  - Description: Machine learning cluster assignments for users.

- **`kpi.daily_funnel`**
  - Columns: `event_date`, `total_views`, `total_carts`, `total_purchases`, `total_removes`, `view_to_cart_pct`, `cart_to_purchase_pct`, `overall_conversion_pct`, `cart_abandonment_pct`
  - Description: Dynamic rolling 30-day daily conversion funnel based on the latest database timestamp.

- **`kpi.top_products_30d`**
  - Columns: `product_id`, `product_name`, `price`, `total_views_30d`, `total_carts_30d`, `total_purchases_30d`, `total_removes_30d`, `total_revenue_30d`, `conversion_rate_30d`
  - Description: Top-performing products over the last 30 days ordered by revenue.

- **`kpi.top_brands_30d`**
  - Columns: `brand_id`, `brand_name`, `total_views_30d`, `total_carts_30d`, `total_purchases_30d`, `total_removes_30d`, `total_revenue_30d`, `conversion_rate_30d`
  - Description: Top-performing brands over the last 30 days ordered by revenue.

- `public.sessions.city_id` $\rightarrow$ `public.cities.city_id`
- `public.products.brand_id` $\rightarrow$ `public.brands.brand_id`
- `public.products.category_id` $\rightarrow$ `public.categories.category_id`
- `public.products.seller_id` $\rightarrow$ `public.sellers.seller_id`
- `public.user_behavior_logs.session_id` $\rightarrow$ `public.sessions.session_id`
- `public.user_behavior_logs.product_id` $\rightarrow$ `public.products.id`
- `public.comments.product_id` $\rightarrow$ `public.products.id`
- `public.comments_embedding.id` $\rightarrow$ `public.comments.id`
- `public.comment_aspects.comment_id` $\rightarrow$ `public.comments.id`
- `kpi.ml_user_clusters.user_id` $\rightarrow$ `public.users.user_id`
